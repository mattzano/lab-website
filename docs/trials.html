Clinical trials

We run phase 2 and 3 trials in ALS and spinal and bulbar muscular atrophy (SBMA, Kennedy’s disease) at the Motor Neuron Disease Centre of Azienda Ospedale Università Padova, and coordinate the clenbuterol trial in SBMA.

2026

ALS · Phase 3

PREVAILS

A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of the efficacy and safety of pridopidine in people with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

Expected to randomise: 10

2025

ALS · Phase 2/3

ALCALS (acetyl-carnitine)

A randomised study of the biological and clinical effects of acetyl-carnitine in people with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis.

Patients in the study: 3 · Expected to randomise: 10

2023

SBMA · Coordinating centre

Clenbuterol in SBMA

A placebo-controlled study of the efficacy and safety of clenbuterol in people with spinal and bulbar muscular atrophy. Our centre coordinates the trial.

Completed: 27 · Still in the study: 3

SBMA · Phase 2

NIDO 361-002

A randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of NIDO-361 in people with spinal and bulbar muscular atrophy (Kennedy’s disease).

Completed: 5

ALS · Phase 3b · Open-label extension

PHOENIX-OLE (A35-011)

An open-label extension of PHOENIX on the safety and tolerability of AMX0035 (sodium phenylbutyrate and taurursodiol) for up to 108 weeks.

Randomised: 6 · Completed: 5

ALS · Phase 3 · Open-label extension · Suspended by the sponsor, January 2024

ADOREXT (FAB122-CT-2201)

A multinational open-label extension on the long-term safety of oral FAB122 (edaravone) 100 mg once daily for people previously enrolled in ADORE.

Randomised: 3 · Completed: 3

2022

ALS · Phase 3

ADORE (FAB122-CT-2001)

ALS Deceleration with ORal Edaravone: a multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of oral FAB122 once daily.

Randomised: 6 · Completed: 3

ALS · Phase 3

PHOENIX (A35-004)

A multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study of AMX0035 (sodium phenylbutyrate and taurursodiol) for 48 weeks.

Randomised: 10 · Completed: 8

ALS · Phase 3

Masitinib (AB19001)

A multicentre, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group study of masitinib with riluzole versus placebo with riluzole.

Randomised: 3 · Completed: 1

Trial facilities

Clinical areas

  • 2 rooms for examination and for blood and biological sample collection
  • 1 room, with a bed, for administering investigational products
  • 1 waiting area, also used for post-dose monitoring and safety assessments
  • Rooms and waiting areas close to emergency medical services

Laboratory

  • On-site certified external laboratory for routine and protocol-required analyses
  • 2 centrifuges and biosafety cabinets
  • −20 °C and −80 °C freezers for biological samples
  • Biobanking for long-term sample storage, on request
  • Equipment calibration programme
  • Access to an Electronic Data Capture (EDC) system
